# Bubu!Tea – ERPNext Implementation

## Overview

Bubu!Tea is a simulated beverage retail business established for an Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) implementation project using ERPNext.

The project integrates four major business functional areas: Accounting and Finance, Human Resources, Marketing and Sales, and Supply Chain Management. It demonstrates how an enterprise information system centralises business data, manages departmental operations and supports integrated business workflows.

## Business Background

Bubu!Tea is a beverage brand offering milk tea, fruit tea and cold brew tea products. Its business model includes physical retail operations, takeaway services and online sales.

ERPNext was configured to support the company's business operations, manage organisational resources and facilitate coordination between departments.

## Key Modules

| Module | Implementation |
|---|---|
| Accounting and Finance | Chart of accounts, journal entries and financial records |
| Human Resources | Employee records, salary structures, attendance, leave policies and expense claims |
| Marketing and Sales | Customer management, product information, quotations and sales transactions |
| Supply Chain Management | Supplier management, purchasing and inventory management |
| User Administration | User accounts, departmental roles and access permissions |
| Website | Homepage, product catalogue and individual product pages |

## Business Workflows

Five business workflows were configured and demonstrated within the ERP system:

- **Attendance Workflow** – Employee attendance management.
- **Leave Application Workflow** – Employee leave requests and approval processes.
- **Expense Claim Workflow** – Expense submission, review and approval.
- **Purchase Workflow** – Procurement and supplier-related transactions.
- **Sales Workflow** – Customer-related sales transactions and processing.

These workflows demonstrate the coordination of business activities between employees, managers and functional departments.

## Project Scope and Limitations

This project is an academic simulation that demonstrates the practical implementation and configuration of an existing ERP platform within a simulated business environment.

It focuses on enterprise system configuration, business process integration and workflow management rather than developing an ERP platform from scratch.
